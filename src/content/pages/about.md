I'm Yogesh Paliyal, a software engineer from Jodhpur, Rajasthan, now based in Bengaluru. I'm a **Software Development Engineer III – Mobile** at [Flipkart](https://flipkart.com/), and I've been building Android apps since 2017, without a computer science degree.

At Flipkart I'm part of **Apps Core**, working on React Native internals and the app's performance and stability. Outside work I'm an **Editor at [ProAndroidDev](https://proandroiddev.com/)**, and I build open-source Android apps like [Halo](/projects/halo), [SendAhead](/projects/sendahead) and [KeyPass](https://github.com/yogeshpaliyal/KeyPass).
