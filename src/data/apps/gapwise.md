## For the things that don't run on a schedule

When did you last get a haircut? Change the car's oil? Call home? Gapwise keeps the answer one tap away. It learns your usual gap between each one and nudges you around when it's usually due.

## Private

No account, ads or tracking. Back up with JSON export or to your own Google Drive's hidden app folder, which only Gapwise can read.

## Built with Material 3 Expressive

Each event's icon sits in a shape that shows where it is in its cycle: a circle when fresh, a scallop as it builds, a sun when it's due, and a soft burst once it's past your usual.
