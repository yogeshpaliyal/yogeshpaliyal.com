## Schedule WhatsApp messages, with attachments

Birthday wishes at midnight, a reminder to a client first thing Monday, a file sent while you're on a flight: write it now and SendAhead sends it at the right time.

## Fully offline

There's no internet permission, no account and no server. Messages and files stay on your phone until WhatsApp sends them.

## How it works

1. An exact alarm fires at the time you picked.
2. SendAhead opens WhatsApp with the chat and message filled in.
3. Its accessibility service taps **Send**, then returns to the home screen. A notification tells you whether it was sent.

## Setup

1. Install the APK and open SendAhead.
2. Tap **Enable** and turn on *SendAhead auto-send* in Accessibility settings. On Android 13+, you may need *App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings* first.
3. Allow **Alarms & reminders** so messages go out on time.
4. On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and similar phones, set battery usage to *Unrestricted*.
