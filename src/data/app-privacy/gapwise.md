Gapwise is an Android app by Yogesh Paliyal for tracking how long it's been since you last did something. In short: **Gapwise has no account, no ads and no tracking, and the developer never receives your data.**

## What Gapwise keeps on your phone

Your events, categories, logs, notes and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

## Backups you control

- **JSON export and import:** you choose where the file goes.
- **Google Drive backup (optional):** if you turn it on, Gapwise saves a backup to the hidden *app data* folder of your own Google Drive (the `drive.appdata` scope). Only Gapwise can read that folder, and it doesn't appear among your Drive files. The backup is stored by Google under Google's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

Gapwise uses the internet only for this optional Google Drive backup.

## Permissions

| Permission | What Gapwise uses it for |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Gentle nudges when something is usually due. |
| Internet | Only for the optional Google Drive backup and restore. |

## Analytics, ads and tracking

Gapwise has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code.

## Children

Gapwise isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
