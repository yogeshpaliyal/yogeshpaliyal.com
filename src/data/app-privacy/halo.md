Halo is a floating app switcher for Android, developed by Yogesh Paliyal. In short: **Halo does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any of your data.** The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

## What Halo keeps on your phone

Halo saves only its own settings, in the app's private storage on your device:

- the apps you picked as favourites, and their order
- where the bubble sits on the screen, and whether it's turned on
- your gesture settings: tap action, hold delay and haptics

This information never leaves your phone through Halo. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

**Android backup:** if Android's device backup is on, Android may include these settings in your phone's backup to your Google account. That backup is run by Android and Google under Google's privacy policy, not by Halo. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

## Permissions and why Halo needs them

| Permission | What Halo uses it for |
| --- | --- |
| Display over other apps | Draws the bubble and the app menu on top of other apps. |
| Usage access (optional) | Finds which apps you opened recently, to fill the outer ring of the menu, and, only if you pick apps to hide the bubble in, which app is on screen. Read on your phone as needed; never saved or sent. Without it, Halo shows favourites only. |
| Foreground service | Keeps the bubble running while it's turned on. |
| Notifications | Shows a silent notification while the bubble is on, with a Stop button. |
| Run at startup | Turns the bubble back on after a restart or update, only if it was on before. |
| Notification access (optional, off by default) | Only to know which apps have notifications waiting, so Halo can show a dot. Halo never reads, stores or sends what a notification says. |
| Photo picker (optional) | Only if you choose "Your photo" for the bubble. Android shares just the one image you pick, and Halo keeps a small 256 px copy in its private storage. |
| List installed apps | Shows your apps so you can choose favourites, and opens the app you pick. The list stays on your phone. |

Halo also keeps a **Recap tally on your phone**: which apps you open with Halo, the hour and the gesture, as daily counts for the last 62 days. It's used only for your Recap, never uploaded, and you can reset it from the Recap.

Halo has no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or accessibility permissions.

## Analytics, ads and tracking

Halo has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and no account or sign-in.

## Where you download Halo

The store or site you install Halo from may collect information under its own policy. For example, Google Play and GitHub keep download statistics. As the developer, I only see totals, such as the number of installs, never who you are.

## Children

Halo isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
